## Latent hold — chained clips stay clean now 🚀

So the big one. When you chain clips with CONTINUE FROM, the image used to get duller and crunchier at every link. I ran the same jazz shot over 6 clips to check: −14% saturation and +55% edge crunch by the end. Super visible.

I thought it was exposure, so I tried pulling the guide back toward the reference. Didn't help at all. The real cause was the round trip: every link decoded the previous clip to pixels and re-encoded it, and each pass cooked it a bit more.

Now H3 Studio continues from the previous clip's **own latent** instead. Same 6-clip test: brightness, contrast, saturation and detail flat. There's still a little drift from the model itself, but the picture stays clean.

**How to use it**
- Wire your **first sampler's LATENT** (SamplerCustomAdvanced `output`) into the new `latent` input on **Glide Video**. Every render then saves a `.h3latent` file next to the video.
- Latent hold is **on by default**. If a clip has no `.h3latent`, it falls back to the normal guide by itself.
- Careful: take the latent from the first pass. A refine pass on an upscaled latent has a different size and won't be used.

**Cleaning up**
- Each `.h3latent` is about 30 MB. ⚙ Settings → **Delete latents…** removes them for the current project once the chain is finished. Your videos are never touched.
