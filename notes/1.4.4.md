## Upscale a take later — Glide Latent Load 🔍

So the upscale pass used to run on every render, including the takes I end up throwing away. Now you can split it: draft at canvas size, keep the good ones, upscale only those later.

- **Glide Video → new `save_latent` option** (only used when the `latent` input is wired):
  - **tail** (default) — `.h3latent`, ~5 MB, for continuing linked clips. Same as before.
  - **full** — also saves `.h3full`, the whole latent (~32 MB), so you can upscale that take later.
  - **off** — saves nothing.
- **New node: Glide Latent Load.** Lists every `.h3full` in your output folder, newest first, and gives it back as a normal LATENT. Plug it into your latent upscaler and refine sampler — no first pass to re-run, and no re-encoding the video.
- For the prompt, open the same clip in H3 Studio and wire `positive_refine` into the refine sampler.
- **Delete latents** never removes `.h3full` files, only the chaining tails.

Careful: upscaling linked clips one by one can make the seams show again, each clip gets refined on its own. Fine for single clips and cuts.
