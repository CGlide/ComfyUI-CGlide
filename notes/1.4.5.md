## Upscale pass — draft first, upscale the keepers later 🔍

> ⚠️ **H3 Studio's outputs changed — rewire once after updating.** I removed the ones nobody plugs in (`width`, `height`, `length`, `seconds`, `guide_frames`) and put `positive` and `positive_refine` together at the bottom. New order: `latent`, `overlap_frames`, `source_video`, `clip_name`, `locked_audio`, `positive`, `positive_refine`. ComfyUI connects wires by position, so old workflows will have them plugged in the wrong place — check the wires on H3 Studio before your first render.

So now you can render your whole project as drafts at canvas size, look at it, and only upscale what you keep. Without re-running the first pass.

**Upscale pass in H3 Studio**
- Render your drafts with `save_latent = full` on Glide Video. That saves the whole latent next to each video as `.h3full`.
- In an upscale workflow (H3 Studio's `latent` → upscaler → refine, no first sampler), tick **upscale** in the Render row and hit Render.
- Each clip is built from its own saved latent. Linked clips get joined onto the **upscaled** render of the clip before, so a chain comes out as one file at upscale size.
- Drafts and upscales are recorded apart, neither overwrites the other.

**New node: Glide Upscale Hold** — goes between Concat AV Latent and the refine sampler.
My first try upscaled each clip on its own and the join was brutal, each refine invents its own texture. This pins the start of the clip to the refined end of the clip before, same idea as latent hold. Tested on a two-clip chain: motion perfect, no hitch. Some details still change between the clips — that's from the drafts themselves.
For it to work, wire the refine sampler's output into Glide Video's `latent` (save_latent = tail), so each upscaled clip saves its refined tail for the next one.

**Glide Latent Load** — the file list now refreshes with a ↻ button, and doesn't reset anymore when you switch between node renderers.

Careful: untick **upscale** before going back to your draft workflow, otherwise it loads the saved latents instead of generating.
