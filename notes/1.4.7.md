## Type < for tags, hover a tag to see its picture 🖼️

Two small things in the H3 Studio prompt.

**< autocomplete** (thanks donyzetti, issue #37)
If you write prompts in the official format, you type <Subject 1>, <Subject 2>... all the time. Now just type <, and the same popup as @ opens:
- the subjects already in your prompt
- the next free one, so <Subject 3> if you already have 1 and 2
- the real <Picture N> / <Video N> / <Audio N> tags of your filled slots, with the same numbers the model will see

Type a few letters to filter it (<sub, <pic). <Subject N> tags are also coloured in the prompt now, like the others.

**Hover preview**
Put your mouse on a coloured tag in the prompt and you get a small preview of the reference, at its real aspect ratio, not a cropped square. Works on @image / @video tokens, on raw <Picture N> / <Video N> tags, and on <Subject N>: it shows the picture the subject is defined from on its line, like "<Subject 1> is the woman in @image1".

Only the front end changed, so a browser refresh is enough. No restart needed.
