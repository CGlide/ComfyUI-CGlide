## H3 Studio outputs are back 🔌

OK, I was wrong on this one. In 1.4.5 I removed `width`, `height`, `length` and `seconds` because I thought nobody plugged them in. Turns out some of you use H3 Studio as the one place that sets resolution and length for the whole workflow, and that's exactly what those outputs were for. Thanks MADmotionAI for calling it out.

So they're back, plus a new one:
- `width`, `height`, `length` (frames), `seconds`
- `refine_multiplier` — the refine-pass reference multiplier from the settings, so you can drive the rest of the refine side from it

They're added **at the bottom**, after `positive_refine`. Your 1.4.5 workflows keep all their wires, nothing to rewire this time.

`guide_frames` stays gone.

In the upscale pass, `width` / `height` / `length` come from the saved `.h3full`, so they match the latent you're actually refining.

Python file changed, so restart ComfyUI.
