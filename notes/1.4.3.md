## Smaller latents, overlap setting, cleaner settings

- **Latent files are 6× smaller.** Glide Video now saves only the tail of the latent — the part latent hold actually uses. About 5 MB per clip instead of 30. Files you rendered before still work, they're just bigger.
- **Continuation overlap** in ⚙ Settings: `22f` (default) or `39f`. 39 carries more motion through the join but re-renders more of the previous clip. Applies everywhere — Render, last render, dropping a clip on CONTINUE FROM, and Lay out clips for the song.
- **Settings panel is compact now** — one line per setting, the explanations are in the tooltips. Hover if you need them.
- README updated.

Careful: `csglide_video.py` changed, so this one needs a **full ComfyUI restart**, not just a browser refresh.
