## Timeline audio — play it, scrub it, drop on it 🎧

Small one, but I use it all the time now.

- **Play button** on the left of the timeline audio lane. You hear this clip's part of the song, with any placed audio on top — the song ducks under each block the same way Glide Join mixes it. Gaps with no song stay silent, that's where H3 makes its own sound.
- **Scrub:** click or drag the ruler (the 1s, 2s… strip) to move the red playhead. When the clip ends, the head jumps back to where you pressed play, so you can loop a section.
- **Drop audio straight on the lane.** It goes into the first empty audio slot, starts where you let go, and switches to Timeline mode if needed.
- **Long audio fits the clip.** Drop a 2 min track and the block now ends at the clip's end, so dragging the right edge shortens it right away. Before, you had to drag back across the whole hidden length before anything moved.
- **× Remove song** spelled out on the song row, instead of a lone cross.
- README updated for projects, links, latent hold, song mode and timeline audio.
- Build version shown in ⚙ Settings.

The node doesn't get taller, the button sits beside the lane.
