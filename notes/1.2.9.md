## New node: Glide DLSS5

Drop a video on the node, scrub to any frame, press **Preview**: a short DLSS 5 burst renders at the playhead and the view becomes a before/after split you slide with the mouse. **Queue** renders the whole file.

- Works with the **DLSS5 Settings** node — Preview always reads its current values, so tweak, preview, compare.
- Preview runs outside the queue: it never starts the other outputs in your graph.
- The scrubber decodes frames on the server, so 4:4:4 HEVC in MKV — the H3 chaining format browsers cannot play — scrubs fine.
- Full renders go through the DLSS5 pack's own video pipeline: same encoder, audio and verification as DLSS5 Enhance Video File.
- **Requires** [ComfyUI-DLSS5-Enhancer](https://github.com/Blueforcer/ComfyUI-DLSS5-Enhancer) (MIT) and its runtime. Glide DLSS5 ships none of it.
- Reads videos only from ComfyUI's input, output and temp folders.

## H3 Studio

- **Links on the timeline.** A chain button on each seam links two clips: the later one continues from the earlier one's last render. Linked runs are drawn joined, with a dashed orange rule in the CONTINUE FROM colour.
- **Render scope** replaces the chained / separate dropdown: this clip, this chain, from this clip on, or the whole project. Rendering two linked clips in the middle of twenty is one click.
- Each clip remembers its last render, so a single link can be re-rolled on its own and a scope can start mid-chain.
- **+ look** now applies to cuts only (unlinked clips), on by default.
- Old projects are migrated from evidence: a clip that already holds a CONTINUE FROM file is linked.
- The timeline is **always visible**, even before a project exists (the current setup shows as Clip 1).
- Timeline and mode bar are the same height; larger text in the clip blocks.
- The UI prints its build in the browser console on load.

## Glide Video

- With `save_metadata` on, writes ComfyUI's native `prompt` and `workflow` tags, so an **MP4 can be dropped on the canvas to reopen its workflow**. The `comment` tag stays for exiftool and VLC.
